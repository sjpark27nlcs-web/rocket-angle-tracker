5. Instructions

Field use:

  1. Open this page directly in Safari/Chrome over HTTPS.
  2. Enter observer position relative to the launch pad.
  3. Tap Enable Sensors.
  4. Point phone/sight at launch pad and tap Calibrate.
  5. Tap Start Logging before launch.
  6. Track the rocket/parachute. Tap markers if possible.
  7. Download CSV and import it into MATLAB.
